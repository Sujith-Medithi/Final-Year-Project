## 2. Findings on Ground Truth Correlation

- **Assertion Frequency (`num_assertions`)**: In miter programs generated by the MQIL algorithm, CBMC checks secret equivalence using assertions. Programs with higher assertion density naturally present higher leakage bounds.
- **Explicit Flows & Memory References**: Direct assignments from high-security inputs to low-security outputs (`num_explicit_flows`) along with secret array indices (`secret_memory_ratio`) are primary drivers of non-zero leakage.
- **Program Scale (`loc`, `num_instructions`)**: Provides the capacity constraint for complex multi-path secret leaks.
